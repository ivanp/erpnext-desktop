2 · Welcome

SSerpy—▢✕

Set up ERPNext on this PC

Serpy will download ERPNext and put it together on this PC. You don't have to do anything while it works.

  • Takes about 1 hour
  • Needs about 30 GB of free space
  • Needs the internet until the downloads finish
  • You can keep using your PC

This PC doesn't have enough free space

Other case, same screen

!

A setting this PC needs is turned off

The setting is called virtualization. It lets Serpy run ERPNext inside Windows. Serpy can't turn it on, because it lives in the PC's startup settings.

What to do: ask the person who looks after this PC to turn on virtualization, then check again.

R5, R40. Setup stops here, before downloading anything. A technical word appears only because the user has to pass it on, and it is explained in one sentence.
